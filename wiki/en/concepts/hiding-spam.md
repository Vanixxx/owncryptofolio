# Hiding spam

## What spam tokens are

On public blockchains, **anyone** can send you tokens without asking. It costs the
sender almost nothing and happens all the time: worthless coins with made-up names land
in your wallet, often with a link in the name meant to lure you to a scam site.

You never bought them. They are worth nothing. Still, they show up in your
transactions because the blockchain reports them correctly.

> **Never interact with such tokens** — do not swap them, do not sell them, do not click
> a link in the name. That is exactly what the scam is aiming for.

## What hiding does

You can hide a single transaction or an entire coin. Anything hidden

- disappears from your tables,
- counts **nowhere**: not in the portfolio value, not in profit and loss, not in the
  tax report,
- but stays in the raw data.

The last point is important: hiding **deletes nothing**. You can undo it at any time,
and the evidence remains.

## How to hide

- **An entire coin**: on the **Prices** page, click "Ignore". This makes it spam
  everywhere, including in transactions and in the balance check.
- **A single transaction**: under **Transactions → Actions → Hide as spam**.
- **Dust**: The program suggests tiny receives (under 0.01 % of what you otherwise
  received of this coin at the source). Under the **Dust** filter you hide them one by
  one or all at once. Nothing is ever hidden automatically, because staking rewards can
  be tiny too, and they count.

Under the **Spam (hidden)** filter you bring a transaction back with "Not spam".

## Why this is more than tidying up

Without hiding, a worthless token with an invented price would flow into your
portfolio value. And the tax report would show a receive that you neither bought nor
wanted.

So hiding is not cosmetic; it affects your numbers — in the right direction.

## What you should check

Only hide what really is spam. A coin you bought yourself and then forgot about belongs
in the calculation. When in doubt, ask yourself: **Did I ever pay anything for it?**
If not, and you did not expect it as a staking reward or airdrop either, it is spam.
