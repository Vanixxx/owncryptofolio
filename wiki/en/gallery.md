# Gallery of owncryptofolio

All pages on the PC and on the phone. The pictures come from a **demo database with
made-up data** (savings plan at Kraken, hardware wallet, a few altcoins at Bitget, a sale
in December 2025) — no real accounts, addresses or amounts. The prices are real daily
prices.

To regenerate: `src/backend/scripts/demo_data.py` creates the demo data in a separate
database (its name must end in `_demo`).
