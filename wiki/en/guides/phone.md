# On your phone

owncryptofolio is built for the phone: open it in the browser, done. There is no app in the
store, and none is needed.

## Like an app on the home screen

- **iPhone (Safari)**: open the page → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome)**: open the page → menu **⋮** → **Add to Home screen** or
  **Install app**.

After that, owncryptofolio starts full screen without an address bar. You choose the icon
under [Settings → App icon](settings.md#app-icon). A new icon only applies once you remove the
old one from the home screen and add the page again.

## On the go

owncryptofolio belongs on your home network. On the go, you connect safely through a **VPN**
to your home (e.g. WireGuard, Tailscale, your router's VPN feature). Putting the page openly
on the internet is not intended.
