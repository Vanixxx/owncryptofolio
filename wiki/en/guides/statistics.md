# Statistics

The **Statistics** page shows how your portfolio has developed over time. All amounts are
in your display currency (Settings).

## Account health

<p>
  <img src="../../bilder/en/details/stat-gesundheit.png" alt="Account health on a PC" width="640">
  <img src="../../bilder/en/details/handy-gesundheit.png" alt="Account health on a phone" width="340">
</p>

Right at the top: how reliable your data is. The ring is green when nothing is open;
otherwise it shows the open points by kind — missing history (gaps), differing balances,
transactions without counterpart, balance below zero, **Other** (rows that are not assigned
to any kind and are usually misclassified), coins without a price (with names; a tap opens
the coin on the Prices page), card purchases without euro amount, bookings **changed by the
source** and failed fetches. Tapping a point takes you to where you resolve it.

Open points come first, with their count; what is in order sits quietly below. On a phone
it is hidden at first ("6 without findings ▾"); tapping the card shows it as small green
tags, with the hint text. On a PC the ring shows the number of open points in the middle.

## Returns per month and year

<p>
  <img src="../../bilder/en/details/stat-rendite.png" alt="Returns per month and year" width="640">
</p>

A colored table: one row per year, the whole year first, then the months. Green is gain,
red is loss; the stronger the color, the more. At the top right you switch between **%**
and **€**.

What counts is the change in value **without** deposits and withdrawals: end value minus
start value minus what you deposited in the period. The percentages refer to the value at
the start plus what was deposited.

## Comparison: everything in BTC

<p>
  <img src="../../bilder/en/details/stat-btc.png" alt="Comparison with BTC" width="640">
</p>

What if you had put every deposit into Bitcoin on the same day and taken every withdrawal
out of Bitcoin? The orange line shows that value, the light one your real value, the dashed
one what you deposited.

## Spot balance

<p>
  <img src="../../bilder/en/details/stat-spot.png" alt="Spot balance" width="346">
</p>

Realized, unrealized and income (with average cost as in the portfolio), plus how many of
your sales were in profit, the best and the worst sale and the gain per month (per sale
with FIFO as in the tax report).

## Futures balance

The result of all futures, the hit rate (positions in profit), the best and the worst
position, and per month the result, funding, fees and exchange bonus.

## Fees

<p>
  <img src="../../bilder/en/details/stat-gebuehren.png" alt="Fees" width="346">
</p>

Trading, network and futures fees at the daily value, per year and exchange/wallet. Funding
is listed with the futures.

## Income

<p>
  <img src="../../bilder/en/details/stat-ertraege.png" alt="Income" width="346">
</p>

Staking and rewards per month, at their value on arrival.

## Activity

How many buys/sells and transfers you had per month, switchable to deposits and
withdrawals.
